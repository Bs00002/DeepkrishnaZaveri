Our Journey

A Legacy Since 1952

"From Akrund in 1952 to Deepkrishna Zaveri in 2026." Our story spans four chapters of dedicated Gujarati jewellery artistry.

1952

AKRUND

Presence established in Akrund, building values of purity and artisan integrity.

1975

DEHGAM

The second branch expanding our heritage craftsmanship to families across the region.

2025

NIKOL

The third branch continuing our trusted tradition into contemporary retail spaces.

2026

DEEPKRISHNA ZAVERI

Current flagship showroom chapter in Sargasan, Gandhinagar, helmed by Jay Soni.
