A Legacy Since 1952

"With a legacy dating back to 1952, Deepkrishna Zaveri represents a timeless appreciation for jewellery, craftsmanship and celebration."

For generations, fine jewellery in Indian culture has transcended adornment—it is a sacred blessing, an heirloom passed through milestone celebrations, and an enduring symbol of love. At Deepkrishna Zaveri, each creation reflects deep reverence for traditional Indian craftsmanship, Gujarati cultural richness, and royal elegance.
