Generational Goldsmith Lineage

A Legacy Since 1952

"From Akrund in 1952 to Deepkrishna Zaveri in 2026."

Under the guidance of Jay Soni, our journey spans four chapters of authentic goldsmith devotion, hand-carved purity, and unbroken customer relationships.

1952

AKRUND

The inception of our family goldsmith business. Master karigars laying the cornerstone of purity, hand-worked bullion, and enduring trust.

1975

DEHGAM

Expansion into regional bridal jewellery. Cultivating generational patron families with 100% hallmarked ornaments and heirloom setting techniques.

2025

NIKOL

A progressive chapter advancing contemporary bridal aesthetics, bespoke CAD karigari, and 92.5 sterling silver ornament lines.

2026

DEEPKRISHNA ZAVERI

The grand flagship chapter at Synergy Space, Sargasan, Gandhinagar — unifying 74 years of heritage into an immersive luxury jewellery destination.
