Family History

OUR ROOTS (1930 – 1952)

The family history traces back to 1930, with Shri Motichand Heerji Morwadiya (Soni) and Shri Bechardas Motichand Morwadiya travelling from Bela, Kutch.

During the 1947 partition, the family returned to India and rebuilt from the ground up with resilient artisanal determination.

In 1952, the family established its presence in Akrund, laying the bedrock for generations of trusted jewellery crafting.
