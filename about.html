Four Chapters

Honoring Roots, Embracing the Future

The roots of our jewellery business trace back to 1952, beginning with fine artisanal dedication in Akrund. Over the decades, each new chapter has preserved our core dedication to gold purity, customer trust, and traditional Gujarati elegance.

In 2026, owner Jay Soni established the Deepkrishna Zaveri showroom in Gandhinagar, bringing forth our complete offerings across 18K, 22K, 24K Gold, 92.5 Silver, bridal ensembles, and bespoke commissions.

1952 — AKRUND

Chapter 01

The first branch and the beginning of the jewellery business, laying down our foundational values of authenticity and artisan honesty.

1975 — DEHGAM

Chapter 02

The second branch, extending our trusted jewellery craftsmanship and nurturing lasting relationships with families across generations.

2025 — NIKOL

Chapter 03

The third branch, carrying forward our heritage into contemporary retail spaces and serving modern families with timeless jewellery.

2026 — DEEPKRISHNA ZAVERI

Current Chapter

The fourth branch and current chapter: our flagship showroom at Synergy Space, Sargasan, Gandhinagar. Owned and led by Jay Soni, dedicated to bridal masterpieces, fine gold and silver collections, and bespoke jewellery.
