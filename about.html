The Foundation

OUR ROOTS (1930 – 1952)

Our foundation was laid in 1930 by our great-grandfather, Shri Motichand Heerji Morwadiya (Soni), and grandfather, Shri Bechardas Motichand Morwadiya, traveling from Bela, Kutch.

During the 1947 partition, choosing principles over possessions, the family returned to India to rebuild from ground zero.

Through grit and unyielding ethics, Shri Bechardas’s five sons established our presence in Mumbai before returning home to Gujarat in 1952, anchoring our trade in Akrund.

Generational Journey

THE EXPANSION OF EXCELLENCE

How four generations turned a humble artisan workshop into an internationally trusted jewellery house.

Deep Jewellers (Dehgam, 1975)

Established 1975

Founded by Shri Khengardas Bechardas and propelled forward by Shri Kanaiyalal and Shri Hiteshbhai, transforming the brand into a hallmark of credibility. Today, Jinesh, Dishank, and Manav (Ziva Jewels, Nikol) take this dynamic vision global.

Krishna Jewellers (Akrund, 1996)

Heritage Handcraft

Established by Shri Mahendrabhai (Lalbhai), son of Shri Prabhudas Bechardas. Renowned for supreme handcraftsmanship, Lalbhai set the industry benchmark for authentic, heritage-rich 'Rabari Ghat' ornaments across the region.

The Modern Era (2017 – Present)

Global Reach

Helmed by Jay Soni and joined by Jeet Soni (2022), blending generational jewelry-making techniques with modern technology. Today, bespoke pieces are delivered directly to patrons across the USA, Canada, UK, Australia, Denmark, Kuwait, UAE, and Africa.
